## Why this bike

The CYC stack (73 mm, ~51 mm chainline, 32T/53T, 12×148, plus tires, 180–203 mm rotors) is an **MTB kit**. A Karate Monkey XL is that bike. A Straggler is a good *adaptation* (drops, 50/20, 135 QR); this is the hole the motor was drawn for.

Rider: 190 cm / 6'3", 300 lb. 42/20 is the lowest comfortable pedal gear (Kilo, throttle-blip hills, no lug). 50/20 is the honest all-day gear (Madison 44/16 felt quick, then beat the knees). Drop bars / hoods are **not** the plan on this frame — keep the Salsa Rustler. Full-twist throttle replaces the right grip (CYC includes a matching left). 22.2 mm Rustler is the right diameter.

### Why 32T, not 40T

Surly KM **1× Boost max is 32T**. Forum: 34–36T only if the ring is moved *outboard*. The Fool’s Gold complete already runs a 32T. CYC 40T/72T is a **~184 mm** motor sprocket + cover — that is the yoke-hitter; do not order it.

32T/53T (~136 mm gold sprocket, cover **~140 mm**) is the kit that matches the frame. Leftover check when the complete is in hand: the **cover** is a few millimetres bigger than a naked 32T, and CYC 73 mm chainline is **50.5 mm** (a hair inboard of Boost 52 mm). If the cover kisses the yoke, pull it or run a 30T 104 BCD later. The motor still goes in. Paper-template at the BB; not a gate on the order.

The **12-speed 11–51** is how 50/20 comes back:

On **27.5×3.0** (~2.30 m circ), 90 rpm:

| 32T × | Ratio | ~mph @ 90 rpm | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| **13T** | 2.46 | **~19** | **50/20** |
| 15T | 2.13 | ~16 | 42/20 floor |
| 11T | 2.91 | ~22 | Madison — optional, not all-day |
| 18T+ | ≤1.78 | spinny | ignore under power |

Live in **13 and 15**. PAS on 13T ~**25 mph** @ 120 rpm. Throttle will outrun the brakes if you let it; ride conservative. PAS off or 0 in Ride Control — throttle is the go-pedal.

**Later ring swaps:** the 32T is 104 BCD — any 104 BCD ring (38T/40T) can go on the **same 53T** later (taller gear, small gold sprocket). Full **40T/72T** still needs 184 mm at the yoke; if 32T was chosen because 72T doesn’t fit, 72T will not fit later either.

**CYC 5-speed cassette:** skip. Stock Microspline 12-speed stays. The CYC HD 15–30 is **Shimano HG** — it will not slide onto the Novatec Microspline freehub. Buying the $179 cassette (or the $369 kit with an Alex 12×148 HG hub) is a **new rear wheel**. Roll off to shift; don’t dump 70 A on 11T or 51T. When the 12-speed sharks or a spoke goes, *then* the 5-speed.

## Boost 148 (why this frame)

6 mm wider than 142, 12 mm thru-axle. Wider flanges → stiffer wheel, less dish, ~52 mm 1× chainline, plus-tire clearance. CYC HD hub is 12×148. Thru-axle will not walk like a 135 QR under 280 Nm. Native, not adapted.

## Pack / safety

Same 14S 21700, 70 A cap, XT90-S, DMM before mate, one-hand rule. Diagnose group 3 before 70 A. Floating negative unless later bonded.

## Setup (draft)

1. Fool’s Gold XL arrives. Sit on it. Return if the 660 mm TT is absurd (you pay freight).
2. Paper-template 32T/53T at 100% at the BB. Cover vs yoke is the only leftover. If it kisses, pull the cover or a 30T 104 BCD later.
3. Pull stock crank/BB. CYC in clean 73 mm shell. Re-torque clamp after ride 1 and week 1.
4. Pull both stock grips. Full-twist on the right, matching CYC grip on the left. Stack inboard → outboard: **Deore → Tektro → full-twist**. Slide the lever in if the CYC body is longer than the stock grip. Dry-fit before cutting hose. **Keep ISO brakes** (right = rear). Do not swap to motorcycle front-on-the-right.
5. SW102 on the Rustler. No brake sensors.
6. Pack low in the triangle, XT90-S.
7. Ride Control: 70 A, **soft torque ramp**, PAS off or 0. Live in cassette 13T/15T. Roll off to shift. Wrist **flat or below** the grip (a high wrist + a bump is whiskey throttle into 70 A / 280 Nm). Power the display off when you get off the bike.
8. Bolt check, weekly. Rear tension after the first hard rides.
