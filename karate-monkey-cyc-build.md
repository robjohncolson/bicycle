## Why this bike

The CYC stack (73 mm, ~51 mm chainline, 32T/53T, 12×148, plus tires, 180–203 mm rotors) is an **MTB kit**. A Karate Monkey XL is that bike. A Straggler is a good *adaptation* (drops, 50/20, 135 QR); this is the hole the motor was drawn for.

Rider: 190 cm / 6'3", 300 lb. 42/20 is the lowest comfortable pedal gear (Kilo, throttle-blip hills, no lug). 50/20 is the honest all-day gear (Madison 44/16 felt quick, then beat the knees). Drop bars / hoods are **not** the plan on this frame — keep the Salsa Rustler. Thumb throttle is 22.2 mm and clamps the riser natively.

### Why 32T, not 40T

Surly KM **1× Boost max is 32T**. Forum: 34–36T only if the ring is moved *outboard*. CYC 40T/72T is a **~184 mm** motor sprocket + cover — assume it does **not** clear the yoke until a 1:1 printout says yes.

32T/53T (~136 mm gold sprocket) is the kit that fits. The **12-speed 11–51** is how 50/20 comes back:

On **27.5×3.0** (~2.30 m circ), 90 rpm:

| 32T × | Ratio | ~mph @ 90 rpm | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| **13T** | 2.46 | **~19** | **50/20** |
| 15T | 2.13 | ~16 | 42/20 floor |
| 11T | 2.91 | ~22 | Madison — optional, not all-day |
| 18T+ | ≤1.78 | spinny | ignore under power |

Live in **13 and 15**. PAS on 13T ~**25 mph** @ 120 rpm. Throttle will outrun the brakes if you let it; ride conservative.

**Later ring swaps:** the 32T is 104 BCD — any 104 BCD ring (38T/40T) can go on the **same 53T** later (taller gear, small gold sprocket). Full **40T/72T** still needs 184 mm at the yoke; if 32T was chosen because 72T doesn’t fit, 72T will not fit later either. Template both before spending.

**CYC 5-speed cassette:** skip for now. Stock Microspline 12-speed stays. Roll off to shift; don’t dump 70 A on 11T or 51T. When the 12-speed sharks or a spoke goes, *then* the 5-speed.

## Boost 148 (why this frame)

6 mm wider than 142, 12 mm thru-axle. Wider flanges → stiffer wheel, less dish, ~52 mm 1× chainline, plus-tire clearance. CYC HD hub is 12×148. Thru-axle will not walk like a 135 QR under 280 Nm. Native, not adapted.

## Pack / safety

Same 14S 21700, 70 A cap, XT90-S, DMM before mate, one-hand rule. Diagnose group 3 before 70 A. Floating negative unless later bonded.

## Setup (draft)

1. Buy Fool’s Gold XL complete. Sit on it. Return if the 660 mm TT is absurd.
2. Paper-template 32T/53T at the BB.
3. Pull stock crank/BB. CYC in clean 73 mm shell. Re-torque clamp after ride 1 and week 1.
4. Thumb + SW102 on the Rustler. Sensors on the Tektro levers.
5. Pack low in the triangle, XT90-S.
6. Ride Control: 70 A, conservative torque ramp. Live in cassette 13T/15T. Roll off to shift.
7. Bolt check, weekly. Rear tension after the first hard rides.
